Before you start

Right-size this for your program

This works whether you have eight children or two hundred, whether you run a home-based program or a multi-site organization, and whether you have a full leadership team or it is mostly you.

One question first. Who is the person closest to running things when you are not there?

Exercise 1

Score all twenty-five

For each one, can the people in your program do this without you? YES is 0 dependency points, SOMETIMES is 1, NO is 2. Mark N/A for anything your program genuinely does not do. Your score adjusts to only what applies to you.

Who counts as “your team”

Whoever it actually is. A Director. A lead teacher. Two teachers. One assistant. A substitute. A family member who helps on Fridays.

If you are the only adult in your program most days, score each line this way instead: if I were out sick tomorrow, would this happen correctly without me? That is the same question, and for a solo operator it is the more honest one.

What the score actually means

Every SOMETIMES and every NO represents something your program may still be asking YOU to carry.

And notice: not one of these twenty-five requires an owner. Not one. They require a written process and somebody who owns it. That somebody can be a teacher.

Your goal isn’t to answer faster. Your goal is to eventually make the question unnecessary.

Exercise 2

Pick your first three

Do not document all twenty-five. You will burn a weekend and change nothing. Choose the three biggest NOs, the ones that cost you the most time or carry the most risk, and install those first.

The one that interrupts you most often
A process without an owner goes stale in ninety days. In a small program the owner may be a lead teacher, an assistant, or you by choice.
Pick a real date
One more test before you call it done

A system is a written process that somebody owns, that people can find in under a minute, that gets used when the pressure is on, and that gets updated when it stops working.

If any one of those four is missing, you wrote a document.
